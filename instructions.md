THE ASSIGNMENT
You will build a small Employee Attendance & Analytics API with Python, FastAPI and MongoDB: punch-in and punch-out with business rules, manual corrections with an audit trail, and a few analytics endpoints built on MongoDB aggregation pipelines. You will also review a short piece of existing code and explain a few of your design decisions.

Everything you need is in the kit: the problem statement, the API contract (OpenAPI), the data model with sample data, a starter app, and two short templates.
